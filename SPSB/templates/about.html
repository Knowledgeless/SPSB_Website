Society for the Popularization of Science, Bangladesh (SPSB)

Welcome to the official website of the Student Psychological Support Board (SPSB) at NIT Trichy. We are a dedicated team of volunteers committed to providing psychological support and resources to our fellow students. Our mission is to create a supportive and inclusive environment where students can seek help, share their experiences, and find the resources they need to maintain their mental well-being.

At SPSB, we understand the challenges that students face in their academic and personal lives. We are here to offer a listening ear, guidance, and access to professional resources. Our team is trained to provide confidential support and connect students with the appropriate services on campus. We believe that mental health is just as important as physical health, and we are dedicated to promoting awareness and reducing stigma around mental health issues.

Whether you're dealing with stress, anxiety, depression, or simply need someone to talk to, SPSB is here for you. We encourage all students to reach out to us and take advantage of the resources we offer. Together, we can create a stronger, healthier, and more supportive community at NIT Trichy.
